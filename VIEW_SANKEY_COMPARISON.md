## 🎓 Miller's Law Background

**George A. Miller (1956)**: "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two"

> "The span of immediate memory imposes severe limitations on the amount of information we are able to receive, process, and remember. By organizing information into chunks, we can increase the effective capacity of our working memory."

**Application Here**:
- **Problem**: 25 clusters exceed working memory capacity → cognitive overload
- **Solution**: 7 clusters fit perfectly in working memory → optimal comprehension
